Spelled message

A letter is added after it is held steadily for about half a second.

Type or say it

Reference images are the official ISL A, B and C signs. Letters outside A, B and C are not supported.

Teach the vocabulary (A, B, C)

Best accuracy: record each letter in several lighting conditions and distances. Use the organiser’s offline ISL A/B/C images via upload. Record a separate session as held-out test so your accuracy is honest.

Held-out accuracy

Claims are limited to the tested vocabulary: ISL letters A, B and C for one spelling-help setting. SignSync does not translate ISL in general. Hand tracking runs in your browser; no video leaves your device.
